OUR STORY

Engineering a Better Tomorrow.

The Philippine Society of Agricultural and Biosystems Engineering – Pre-Professional Group, Caraga State University Chapter brings students together through professional development, collaboration, and service.

OUR PURPOSE

Learning with purpose.

We aim to help students connect classroom learning with practical challenges in agriculture, food systems, natural resources, and biosystems engineering.

Through chapter activities, peer learning, leadership opportunities, and community engagement, members can build the knowledge and values needed for their future profession.
